emTasks {{VERSION}}
=============

A quiet to-do organizer for Mac, based on the Eisenhower matrix
(urgent vs. important). Works fully offline. Your tasks stay on your Mac,
apart from a daily CSV backup in Documents/emTasks (synced by iCloud if
your Documents folder is).

Requirements: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 11 or later.

Check the download (optional, recommended)
------------------------------------------
Because emTasks isn't notarized by Apple, check that your copy wasn't
altered. Ask the sender for the SHA-256 fingerprint (ideally through a
different channel than the download), then in Terminal run:

    shasum -a 256 emTasks-{{VERSION}}.zip

The long code it prints must match the fingerprint exactly.

To install
----------
1. Open emTasks-{{VERSION}}.dmg and drag emTasks into Applications.

2. Open emTasks. Because it isn't from the App Store, your Mac will say
   "Apple could not verify emTasks". Click Done.

3. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to emTasks. You only need to do this once.

To update later
---------------
Quit emTasks, then drag the new version into Applications and choose
Replace. Your tasks are kept. They're stored separately from the app.

Back up your tasks anytime with File > Export tasks to CSV.
